Read directly from the official pages on 2026-11-14. Both guides are dated November 13, 2026. If the live pages change, they win over this file.

- Project Submission Guide: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/

## Demo format (the plan asked us to post this)

Pick ONE:

- **Video, 30 seconds or less**, or
- **Slide deck, up to 7 slides including the title and intro slides.**

"Projects that exceed these limits will not be eligible for Global Judging." The demo is hosted outside the Space Apps site (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, OneDrive, Dropbox) and the link must be public with no login, permission or registration. A video may be in another language only if it has English subtitles; slides must be English. A slide demo must show the team name, the team member names, and the challenge chosen. No images, video or audio of anyone under 18.

## Deadline

Submission opens Saturday Nov 14 at 9:00 a.m. and closes **Sunday Nov 15 at 11:59 p.m., in the time zone of the team's Local Event** (Universal Event: the Team Owner's local time). You must click "Submit for Judging" before then; anything saved or submitted later is not eligible. Only the last saved submission is reviewed, and you may resubmit as often as you like until the deadline. Our internal target of 9 PM Sunday leaves a margin.

## Project page fields (all required fields must be filled to submit)

| Field | What the guide asks |
|---|---|
| Project Name | Name of the project |
| High-Level Project Summary | What did you develop, how does it address the challenge, why is it important |
| Link to Project Demo | The video or slide deck above |
| Link to Final Project | Public link to the website, repo or similar, no login |
| Detailed Project Description | What it does, how it works, benefits, goals, tools/languages/software used |
| Use of Super Intelligence (SI) | Whether and how SI tools were used |
| NASA Data | Specific NASA data and resources used (at least one is required to be eligible for a Global Award) |
| Space Agency Partner Data and Other References | Every data source, resource and tool, including all code, text and images, even open source |

The page accepts image uploads (JPG, PNG, GIF, up to 10 MB) but not videos or documents. Two checkboxes are required: the Terms and Conditions, and "Confirm All Work is Original" (all external resources, including code, text and images, must be listed in the References field, and no copyrighted material we lack permission to use).

## SI (AI) rules that affect what we ship

- SI tools are permitted, and the challenge text itself says they could be useful here.
- The guide says SI "should not be used to come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge." The team should be ready to describe accurately who originated the idea.
- Code and data generated with SI "must acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and metadata." Images and video need a visible watermark. Audio needs acknowledgement in text and metadata.
- SI-generated content may not use or modify NASA branding (logos, flags, mission identifiers). Avoid NASA logos in the app and demo altogether.
- The SI answer on the form is for data collection and is not seen by judges, but judges see any SI indicators inside the project and judge originality, intent and execution of SI-assisted parts.

## Judging

Every project is scored 1 to 5 on five criteria at every level: Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation.

- **Impact**: size and quality of effect on the problem, reach and who it helps.
- **Creativity**: originality, a novel concept or a meaningful improvement on existing ideas.
- **Validity**: scientifically grounded, technically feasible, usable and user-friendly.
- **Relevance**: directly addresses the challenge and uses NASA data as a core component, not an add-on.
- **Presentation**: clear storytelling. "Judges evaluate the demo and full project to compute this score." The guide urges equal attention to this criterion.

Process: Local judges pick Global Nominees (announced Dec 3, 2026), global expert judges pick Finalists (Dec 17, 2026), an executive committee picks winners for ten Global Awards (announced Jan 26, 2027). Candidates for us: Best Use of Science, Best Use of Data, Galactic Impact, Best Storytelling.

## Eligibility checklist for the team

- Respond to one official challenge (ours: Be An Earth System Trend Detective).
- Team of at most six. Every member registered, at the same Local Event, not waitlisted, and listed on the Members tab. No new members after submission closes.
- English everywhere, except a video voice-over with subtitles.
- Teams go through NASA's review for prohibited bilateral activity with China or Chinese-owned entities to be eligible for Global Judging (details in the guide and Terms and Conditions).
- If we host a new website, the guide recommends requesting a FortiGuard Web Filter Classification so judges' networks do not block it. Not required, but it prevents access trouble.
